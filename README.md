# Luxury Salon — Web Application & Design System

A responsive, high-converting luxury hair studio and appointment scheduling website template designed for **Luxury Salon** (*Hair Studio & Spa*). Built as a real-world client solution and a flagship UX/UI and Frontend Engineering portfolio project.

---

## ✨ Key Implemented Features

1. **Interactive Monthly Studio Calendar (`calender dekhabe`)**:
   - Live monthly calendar view calculating days, weekdays, and current selection.
   - Interactive date clicks with instant available time slot pills (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
   - Past/closed dates gracefully disabled.

2. **Seamless Booker Modal (Option A)**:
   - Dedicated 4-step appointment scheduling modal matching the reference app screens.
   - Seamlessly preserves brand immersion on the website without sending visitors to a jarring third-party redirect.
   - Ready to integrate with **Booker by Mindbody** or custom scheduling backends.

3. **Curated Luxury Brand Visuals (FreshCut Palette)**:
   - Deep Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`), Soft Sage (`#E8F3EE`), Crisp White, and Slate Charcoal.
   - 100% white-label placeholders for services, stylists, reviews, boutique products, address, and interactive map.

4. **Interactive "Before & After" Lookbook Slider**:
   - Touch- and drag-enabled split-screen image slider to showcase hair and beard transformations.

5. **Signature Botanical Haircare Boutique**:
   - Retail product grid with slide-over shopping bag drawer and subtotal calculations.

6. **Mobile-First Experience**:
   - Responsive bottom app bar navigation for mobile viewports, replicating the native mobile app feel.

## 🚀 How to View & Test Locally

You can open and test this application immediately without installing any server or package manager:

1. Navigate to:
   `C:\Users\User\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\luxury-salon`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, or **Safari**.
3. Test all interactions:
   - Click dates on the **Studio Calendar** to see time slots adapt.
   - Click **Book Appointment** to test the 4-step modal.
   - Drag the **Before & After slider** left and right.
   - Click `+` on boutique products to open the shopping bag drawer.
